Good to know

Before we come over.

What are the minimum order and extra fees?

The minimum food order is $450, with a $100 travel fee per event. Omakase requires 6-12 guests at $180 per person, with a minimum food total of $1,080. A server can be added for $150 to the private sushi dinner or omakase. The $550 dinner for two and $800 family dinner already include a server. Birthday, corporate and wedding catering has no server service. Optional on-site sushi preparation adds $200 per event, in addition to the $100 travel fee. A mixology bartender can be added for $150 per event, separate from server service. Alcohol is not included. Taxes and agreed upgrades are additional.

What do I need to provide?

A clear kitchen workspace, running water, and dining space for your guests. We provide chopsticks, soy sauce, wasabi, and ginger. The host provides plates. We’ll confirm refrigeration, access, linens, glassware, and any other rental or equipment needs while planning your event. Dinner-for-two and family packages include table setup and drink service with a dedicated server.

Can you accommodate guests who don’t eat raw fish?

Yes, cooked and vegetarian selections can be included in the agreed menu. Share allergies before booking so we can discuss ingredients and preparation; an allergen-free environment cannot be guaranteed.

Is alcohol included?

No. Alcohol is not included in the sushi package. Any proposed service involving host-supplied alcohol must be confirmed separately for your gathering and location.

How do I confirm a booking?

Preparing or texting an inquiry does not reserve a date. Availability, your menu, the final quote, deposit and cancellation terms must be agreed upon before confirmation.

Where do you travel?

Our planned service area is Myrtle Beach and the Grand Strand, including North Myrtle Beach and Conway. Ask about your location; a $100 travel fee applies per event. Service availability for your address is confirmed before booking.

Consuming raw or undercooked seafood may increase your risk of foodborne illness, especially if you have certain medical conditions.
