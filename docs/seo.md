# Coast Sushi Services search visibility

Production: https://coastsushiservices.com/

The static homepage is the production entry point. Keep its title, visible service descriptions, contact details and Organization/Service JSON-LD consistent with approved business information. The older React package page is temporarily noindex until issue #20 resolves its outdated content. Only the homepage belongs in the sitemap today.

## Owner setup

1. Verify the domain in Google Search Console using the business owner’s account and the verification method Google supplies. Never invent verification tokens. Submit https://coastsushiservices.com/sitemap.xml and inspect/request indexing for the homepage.
2. Claim or verify the Google Business Profile. Use the actual business name, correct service categories, real service areas, phone, website and business hours. If customers do not visit the business address, follow Google’s service-area-business setup and address visibility rules.
3. Add approved food and event photos; ask actual customers for honest reviews without incentives. Keep public contact details consistent.
4. Seek relevant local referrals and links from real event venues, planners and partners. Do not buy links or generate duplicate city pages.
5. Review Search Console impressions, clicks, queries and indexing monthly. Track “sushi catering Myrtle Beach,” “private sushi chef Myrtle Beach,” and relevant North Myrtle Beach/Conway searches using real performance data rather than promising a position.

## Verification

Check canonical, metadata, sitemap, robots and JSON-LD in production. The structured data intentionally uses Organization and Service: no unverified street address, hours, ratings or storefront are claimed. This does not establish eligibility for Google LocalBusiness rich results. Use Google Rich Results Test and Search Console after deployment; Organization/Service graph validity is distinct from rich-result eligibility.
