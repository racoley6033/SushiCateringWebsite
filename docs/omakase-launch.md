# Private omakase launch plan

## Confirmed direction

Coast Sushi Catering offers a private chef for homes and events across Pawleys Island, Murrells Inlet, Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, and Conway. The base experience is ten courses per guest at $160 per person, featuring specialty, fresh, high-quality sushi including uni, toro, and bluefin tuna. Specific courses depend on sourcing and chef review. Request at least 14 days ahead; the date check uses South Carolina's America/New_York calendar date.

## Follow-up questions for the owner

1. Is Coast Sushi Catering the final name, and should Reed's name, biography, and portrait introduce the chef?
2. Which inbox and phone should receive requests? Should customers receive email, text, or both?
3. What are the minimum and maximum guests, and is there a minimum spend?
4. Does $160 include all ingredients, chef time, plates, setup and cleanup? What tax, travel, service charge or gratuity applies?
5. Which days/start times are available, how long is service, and how early does the chef arrive?
6. Are uni, toro and bluefin guaranteed, or can the chef propose substitutions? Which allergies or cooked-food preferences can be accommodated?
7. What counter, kitchen, water, refrigerator and equipment access does the host need? Can you serve vacation rentals and outdoor events?
8. Is payment due after Reed accepts, or is a deposit needed? What are the cancellation, rescheduling and chef/supplier cancellation terms?
9. Does the two-week cutoff run from the initial request or accepted/paid booking? Are exceptions permitted?

## Marketing recommendations

- Lead with one clear offer: “Private 10-course omakase, at your table. $160 per guest. Pawleys Island · North Myrtle Beach · Conway. Reserve at least two weeks ahead.”
- Photograph an actual ten-course dinner, including uni, toro, bluefin and the chef working at a home counter. Use the existing gallery as a portfolio until then, without implying it is a fixed menu.
- Create short Instagram videos of one course at a time and an evening from setup to final course. Finish with the price, service area and booking lead time.
- Approach vacation-rental managers, concierges, wedding planners and luxury real-estate hosts with a one-page menu and inquiry link. Promote birthdays, anniversaries and group vacation dinners.
- Once booking delivery is live, set up a service-area Google Business Profile and useful local search content for private sushi chefs in Pawleys Island, North Myrtle Beach and Conway. Verify eligibility and avoid duplicate or thin location pages.
- Ask completed-event customers for permission to share photos and genuine reviews. Track qualified inquiries, quotes, confirmed events, average guest count and acquisition cost before expanding paid ads.
- Start marketing toward dates at least three to four weeks away, leaving time for enquiry review and fish sourcing. Keep paid conversion campaigns paused until requests reliably reach the chef.
